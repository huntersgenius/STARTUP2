# Why this matters in Central Asia

Iodine deficiency remains endemic in mountainous and inland regions, including
much of Central Asia, where soil iodine is low. It is the most common
preventable cause of intellectual disability worldwide.

# Recognition

Visible or palpable goitre is the classic sign; palpate the thyroid in every
patient presenting with neck swelling, and in adolescents during routine
examination in endemic areas.

Other features: fatigue, cold intolerance, weight gain, constipation, dry skin,
and in children, poor school performance and growth faltering.

Maternal deficiency in pregnancy causes irreversible neurodevelopmental harm to
the fetus, and is the most important reason to detect and treat it.

# Prevention and treatment

Universal salt iodisation is the primary strategy; counsel every household to
use iodised salt and to store it dry and away from light.

Pregnant and lactating women in deficient areas need 250 mcg of iodine daily.
Supplement where iodised salt coverage is inadequate.

Confirm suspected thyroid dysfunction with TSH before starting levothyroxine.
Goitre alone is not an indication for thyroid hormone.

Refer: a rapidly enlarging goitre, a hard or fixed nodule, hoarseness, or
compressive symptoms including difficulty swallowing or breathing.
