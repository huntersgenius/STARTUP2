# SihhatAI - Executive Summary

**AI-Powered Clinical Decision Support for Central Asia's Largest Healthcare Market**

---

## The Problem

Uzbekistan has the highest diet-related death rate in the world: 892 per 100,000 people annually (Lancet, 2019). With only 27.4 doctors per 10,000 population and 2,834 underequipped rural clinics, millions lack access to quality primary care. Misdiagnosis is endemic, basic medical supplies are frequently unavailable, and patients resort to costly specialists or dangerous self-medication.

## The Solution

SihhatAI is an AI-powered clinical decision support system that helps primary care doctors make faster, more accurate diagnoses. Doctors input patient symptoms in Uzbek or Russian; the AI suggests probable conditions, recommends treatments calibrated to locally available medications, and flags high-risk patients for specialist referral. A hybrid cloud-edge architecture ensures the system works even in clinics with poor internet connectivity.
